About The Folk School

Community vitality through tourism and skill-building.

Mission: Offering VITAL instruction, training, and apprenticeships to under-supported rural populations — and heritage-forward classes, events, and excursions — towards sustainable tourism and community advancement.

Vision: Revitalization of the Rainy Lake area through heritage, skill, coalition-building, and local environmental and agricultural stewardship.

Our North Star

Vital instruction. Heritage-forward learning. Sustainable tourism. Community advancement.

Place-Based Learning

Rooted in Rainy Lake, built for the future.

Ranier and International Falls are a historically vital border town near Voyageurs National Park and the Quetico-Superior boundary waters. The Wharf project builds on the area’s history, natural beauty, community pride, and need for new models of rural vitality.

Fall 2026

Oktoberfest in Ranier

Date: Saturday, October 10, 2026

Time: 1:00–6:00 PM

Location: The Folk School and participating businesses throughout Ranier, Minnesota

Join us for a festive afternoon celebrating Ranier's new businesses, local artists, makers, and the wonderful community along Rainy Lake!

This community celebration brings together The Wharf Folk School & Event Center, Backus Community Center, Cake & Crust Bakery, The Wild Muse, Looney's, and Rainy Lake Inn & Suites at Tara's Wharf.

What to Expect

  • Music, food, and fall festivities
  • Local artists, makers, and vendors
  • Pop-up markets featuring local goods
  • Creative workshops and hands-on activities
  • Opportunities to explore Ranier's new businesses

Workshops at The Wharf

The Wharf will offer hands-on creative activities, including:

  • Mask making
  • Pumpkin painting
  • Possible tack-making and paddle-making workshops
Calling All Artists and Vendors!

Artists, farmers, makers, and local vendors are invited to showcase and sell their goods during Oktoberfest.
