Fall 2026

Community Celebration

Oktoberfest in Ranier

Date: Saturday, October 10, 2026

Time: 1:00 – 6:00 PM

Location: The Wharf and participating businesses throughout Ranier, Minnesota

Join us for a festive afternoon celebrating Ranier's new businesses, local artists, makers, and the wonderful community along Rainy Lake!

This small community celebration is a collaboration among The Wharf Folk School & Event Center, Backus Community Center, Cake & Crust Bakery, The Wild Muse, and Looney's. Rainy Lake Inn & Suites at Tara's Wharf is also a participating location.

What to Expect

  • Music and food
  • Local artists, makers, and vendors
  • Pop-up markets featuring local goods
  • Creative workshops and hands-on activities
  • Opportunities to explore Ranier's new businesses

Workshops at The Wharf

The Wharf will offer hands-on creative activities for visitors of all ages, including:

  • Mask making
  • Pumpkin painting
  • Additional possible workshops in tack-making and paddle making
Calling All Artists and Vendors!

Artists, farmers, makers, and local vendors are invited to showcase and sell their goods during Oktoberfest.

Come share your creativity and help us celebrate the growing Ranier community!

Holiday 2026

Holiday Community Event

Christmas Market

Date: Saturday, December 12, 2026

Location: Backus Community Center

Organized by: Backus Community Center and The Wharf Folk School & Event Center

Celebrate the holiday season with a festive Christmas Market bringing together local artists, musicians, makers, and community members.

Organized in collaboration with Backus Community Center, this special gathering will celebrate creativity, local talent, and the spirit of the season.

Market Highlights

  • A community art exhibit
  • Musical performances
  • Food and hot beverages
  • Local artists and vendors selling their goods
  • A welcoming opportunity to gather with friends and neighbors

Additional information, including event times and vendor details, will be announced as plans are finalized.
