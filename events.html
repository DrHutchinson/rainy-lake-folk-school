Upcoming Events at The Wharf

Join The Wharf Folk School & Event Center for seasonal celebrations, creative workshops, community gatherings, and opportunities to connect with neighbors, artists, makers, and local businesses in Ranier.

Celebrate fall in Ranier with local businesses, food, music, creativity, and community.

Featured Event

Oktoberfest in Ranier

Saturday, October 10, 2026 · 1:00–6:00 PM

Join us for a community-focused Oktoberfest celebrating the new businesses of Ranier and bringing neighbors, visitors, artists, makers, and local organizations together for an afternoon of fall fun.

Location: The Wharf, Rainy Lake Inn & Suites, Looney's, Cake & Crust Bakery, and The Wild Muse
Community Partners: Backus Community Center, Cake & Crust Bakery, The Wild Muse, and Looney's

Enjoy:

  • Music, food, and fall festivities
  • Artists, makers, and local vendors
  • Pop-up markets
  • Creative workshops and hands-on activities

Activities at The Wharf may include:

  • Mask making
  • Pumpkin painting
  • Tack-making
  • Paddle making
Artists and vendors are welcome!
Local artists, makers, farmers, and vendors are invited to show and sell their goods during the celebration.
